About Us

Who We Are

XYZ is a non-profit organization working in West Bengal to provide financial assistance to underprivileged students. We believe that a student's financial circumstances should not prevent them from pursuing education and building a better future.

Our Objective

  • Identify students who need financial assistance for their education.
  • Provide appropriate educational financial support.
  • Encourage students to continue and complete their studies.
  • Promote equal opportunities in education.
